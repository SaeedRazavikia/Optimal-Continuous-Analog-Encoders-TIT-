# Rebuilt deliverables

The earlier local PDF and source ZIP were no longer present when the download failure was reported. This package reconstructs the paper from the attached roadmap, its original proof sources, and the technical development visible in this conversation. It preserves the main results and the projection decoder, but is not a byte-for-byte recovery of the earlier 11-page layout or its numerical scripts.

The rebuilt paper is compiled afresh. Its verification consists of 135 rerun structural checks from the transparently identified supplied checker and 98 new decoder implementation checks. The previous claim of 239 checks refers to the earlier run, whose files are not included here. The results in this package have their own records and provenance.

The original attachment was not modified. The reference_context directory includes the two main supplied proof sources and the roadmap so that the reconstruction can be reviewed independently.
