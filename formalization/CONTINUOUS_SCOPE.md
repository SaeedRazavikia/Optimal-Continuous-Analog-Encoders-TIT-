# Continuous heterogeneous encoders

`HeterogeneousFullRigidity.heterogeneous_rigidity` proves both directions of
the current manuscript's rigidity theorem. Its assumptions are the original
closed-source continuity, interior C1 feature regularity, functional
independence at every node, receiver alternatives on the actual mean domains,
and continuity of the encoders. The conclusion has common feature columns,
separate node offsets, and a nonzero determinant, including both endpoints.

The necessity chain derives physical two-completion separation and density,
local moment recovery, a genuine reverse factorization through planar
invariance of domain, automatic encoder C1 regularity, common derivative
coefficients, endpoint integration and nonsingularity. No density, chart,
encoder derivative, independent-anchor or extra topology hypothesis remains
in the final theorem.

The planar topology proof is included as checked source. Its exact external
provenance and Apache-2.0 attribution are recorded in the completion and
topology provenance notes. No new axiom or admitted theorem substitutes for
invariance of domain.

`HeterogeneousGlobalOptimum.unrestricted_sdp_attained` transfers the attained
SDP optimum to all continuous peak-feasible encoder collections under the
rigidity assumptions. Its competitors need not be assumed exactly decodable:
positive operational score supplies exactness and zero score is bounded by
the nonnegative optimum. The same module connects the actual continuum SDP
and the grid-certification coordinates.

The older shared-curve modules retain their original statements and remain
useful special cases. Their conditional intermediate interfaces are not the
premises of the new final rigidity theorem.
