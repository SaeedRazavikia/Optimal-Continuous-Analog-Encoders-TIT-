# Presentation and formalization revision

The section headings now use short topic names, including System Model,
Encoder Rigidity, Encoder Synthesis, Optimal Curves, and Decoding. Sixteen
routine theorem subtitles were removed; the two retained subtitles highlight
Continuous encoders and Exact frontier. Mathematical assumptions and labels
are preserved.

Table I now has three compact columns: result, shared regime, and additional
requirements. Two short definitions above the table collect the common
rigidity and score assumptions. The previous prose consequence column has
been removed; each theorem remains the authoritative full statement.

The six figures remain native TikZ/PGFPlots, including the multiple-access
system model and the optimal encoder curves. Their source and data remain
editable in the accompanying archive.

Appendix D and the acknowledgment now report the extended verified Lean
scope precisely. They do not claim that all proofs or full continuous-encoder
optimality are formalized. See FORMALIZATION.md and the formalization folder
for the completed declarations and the unresolved dependency chain.
