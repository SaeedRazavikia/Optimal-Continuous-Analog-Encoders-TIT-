\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:boundaries}
We classify exact shared continuous planar encoders for $f_{\phi,\psi}(\mathbf s)=t-\psi(m)$ and optimize their squared separation under pointwise peak power. The resulting convex design and projection decoder attain a sharp uniform disturbance guarantee. Inverse-Gram coordinates also give the robustness-constrained small-noise frontier for the specified decoder, with an explicit quadratic specialization and certified numerical design for smooth preprocessing functions.

The classification assumes one synchronized complex channel use, unit effective gains, a shared encoder, and $K\ge4$. User-specific encoders, unequal uncompensated gains, average-power constraints, and unrestricted $K=3$ classification require further analysis. The two-statistic extension requires common-completion identification for classification, Lipschitz postprocessing for positive separation, and additional endpoint conditions for the explicit quadratic formula. It does not provide a universal representation of symmetric functions~\cite{wagstaff19,wagstaff22}.

For each encoder, the score gives the smallest uniform amplification over exact decoders. The statistical frontier concerns the specified strip decoder; finite-noise Bayesian optimization over arbitrary estimators remains open. Section~\ref{sec:implementation} bounds residual mismatch, while optimal design for unequal channels remains a separate problem.
