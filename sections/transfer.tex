\subsection{Other Dissimilarities}
For a continuous dissimilarity $\mathscr D:[0,\infty)\to[0,\infty)$ and a nonconstant desired function $f:I^K\to\R$, we define
$$
 \Lambda_{\mathscr D}(\enc;f)
 =\inf_{\substack{\mathbf s,\mathbf t\in I^K\\f(\mathbf s)\ne f(\mathbf t)}}
 \frac{\mathscr D\!\left(|r_\enc(\mathbf s)-r_\enc(\mathbf t)|\right)}
 {|f(\mathbf s)-f(\mathbf t)|^2}.
$$
The shrinking-pair statement gives a criterion-transfer corollary over the full continuous competitor class.

\begin{corollary}
Suppose $\mathscr D:[0,\infty)\to[0,\infty)$ is continuous, $\mathscr D(0)=0$, positive away from zero, and
$$
 \mathscr D(d)\ge c_{\mathscr D}d^2\quad(0\le d\le2K\sqrt P),\qquad
 \lim_{d\downarrow0}\frac{\mathscr D(d)}{d^2}=c_{\mathscr D}>0.
$$
Under the hypotheses of Proposition~\ref{prop:score}, every continuous encoder $\enc:I\to\R^2$ that satisfies the peak constraint and the identifiability condition for $f_{\phi,\psi}:I^K\to\R$ satisfies $\Lambda_{\mathscr D}(\enc;f_{\phi,\psi})=c_{\mathscr D}\Lambda_{\sq}(\enc;f_{\phi,\psi})$. Consequently, every optimal encoder for this desired function remains optimal, and the optimal score is multiplied by $c_{\mathscr D}$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The lower envelope gives $\Lambda_{\mathscr D}\ge c_{\mathscr D}\Lambda_{\sq}$. Proposition~\ref{prop:score} supplies pairs attaining the opposite inequality in the limit. Encoders that fail the identifiability condition have zero score because $\mathscr D(0)=0$.
\end{proof}

Examples are $\mathscr D(d)=d^2+ad^4$ with $a\ge0$ and $\mathscr D(d)=e^{ad^2}-1$ with $a>0$. The original uncentered $e^{ad^2}$ criterion has $\mathscr D(0)=1$ and is not covered. No closed-form optimizer for that criterion is claimed here. Gaussian MSE and optimality at a fixed disturbance radius also remain separate problems.
