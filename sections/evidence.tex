\section{Numerical Results}\label{sec:evidence}
\subsection{Analytic Checks}

We compare the scores and statistical performance of the curves in Fig.~\ref{fig:curves}. The analytic proofs establish global optimality; the numerical checks validate coefficients and implementations.

The quadratic baseline is feasible by the peak identity with $b=1$. For the quartic baseline, $x=s^2\in[0,1]$ gives
$$|\enc_{\rm base}(s)|^2/P=\tfrac45[x+(x^2-1/2)^2]\le1,$$
since $x\le1$ and $(x^2-1/2)^2\le1/4$. Its exact score is $(4/85)K^2P$. Table~\ref{tab:values} compares these specified reference designs with the certified optima, without implying MSE gains.

The supplied structural checker verifies the implemented coefficients, quadratic stationarity, quartic peak and multiplier identities, asymmetric mean penalties, shrinking source perturbations, and Gram rank reduction. A separate decoder checker compares the strip formula for nonorthogonal directions with received-plane quadratic programming and verifies exactness and disturbance bounds across several values of $K$ and $P$. These checks pass, but their purpose is implementation verification. The full-interval analytic identities and the rational certificate in Section~\ref{sec:certified} establish feasibility and global bounds independently of sampling.
